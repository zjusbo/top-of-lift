How it's calculated
  1. BL depth zᵢ = the forecast model's boundary-layer height (HRRR, NAM or GFS).
  2. Thermal strength w* = (g/T · H/(ρ·cp) · zᵢ)⅓, where H = surface sensible heat flux.
  3. Updraft w(z) = k · w* · (z/zᵢ)⅓ · (1 − 1.1 z/zᵢ), with k = 1.6. The updraft peaks at about 0.73 · w*, a quarter of the way up the BL.
  4. Top of lift = ground + highest z where w(z) ≥ 1.3 m/s, the glider sink rate.
  5. Launch wind = model wind interpolated to the launch altitude from pressure levels. NAM has none, so it uses the 80 m wind.

k and sink are fixed and not yet calibrated. Compare against your flights before trusting it.
